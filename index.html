Market Leader in Green Feed

Africa's most trusted fodder innovator.

Green Sprouts Solutions is a South African manufacturer of hydroponic fodder systems, biobooster systems, and hothouses, supplying farmers across Africa from our manufacturing facility in George, Western Cape. We have been engineering green feed solutions for over two decades, driven by a singular mission: to make high-quality, nutritious livestock feed accessible and affordable.

Our systems produce year-round greenfeed within 7 days without the need for a pivot or ploughing fields. We partner with university professors to develop precise feeding rations that maximise profit margins for our clients across different geographical regions.

The company was founded by Vivian Beukes, whose engineering background and expertise in bacterial science shaped a product category that has transformed African livestock farming. Green Sprouts Solutions has successfully installed systems all over the country and across the continent.

The Science of Sprouting

Why sprouted fodder works.

🌱

Seed Becomes Feed

A 50kg bag of seed sprouts into approximately 300kg of green nutrient-dense fodder in just 6–7 days. The sprout mat is completely edible — animals eat the entire mat, roots and green growth, so there is zero waste.

300kg Yield from 50kg seed
🧬

Dramatically Improved Digestibility

Barley's digestibility jumps from 41% to 81% through sprouting. Crude protein rises from 12.7% to 15.6% after 7 days of germination. Crude fibre increases from 5.5% to 14.2%, improving gut function in all livestock.

81% Digestibility after sprouting (vs. 41% dry)

Chlorophyll & Vitamin D3

Natural sunlight in our cladding system ensures the plants are loaded with chlorophyll and vitamin D3 — both essential for healthier livestock. Artificial lighting cannot replicate this effect, and it introduces mould risk and electricity cost.

The Technology

Answers to every farmer's question.

No one can argue that artificial lighting cannot compare to natural sunlight in the chlorophyll process. Natural sunlight ensures healthier plants loaded with chlorophyll and vitamin D3 — both essential elements for healthier livestock. By eliminating growing lights, we dramatically reduce electricity running costs and make solar integration far more cost-effective. Artificial lighting also creates environments where unwanted micro-organisms thrive.
SporeBuster is our key organic bacterial product. Combined with our patented cladding technology, it allows the right amount of sunlight to reach the fodder while eliminating mould and fungal growth. A critical additional factor is that our system achieves temperature variation inside the booster — something impossible with a conventional air-conditioner — and this temperature variance is key to eliminating spores at the source.
