The Science of Sprouting

Why sprouted fodder works.

Seed Becomes Feed

A 50kg bag of seed sprouts into approximately 300kg of green, nutrient-dense fodder in 6–7 days. The sprout mat is completely edible — animals eat the entire mat, roots and green growth, zero waste. Commonly used seeds: barley, wheat, maize, whole oats. Barley is the easiest to grow.

300kgYield from 50kg seed in 7 days

Dramatically Improved Digestibility

Barley's digestibility jumps from 41% to 81% through sprouting. Crude protein rises from 12.7% to 15.6% after 7 days. Crude fibre increases from 5.5% to 14.2%. Sprouting also amplifies natural proteins, vitamins, minerals, enzymatic activity, omega-3, amino acids and natural hormones, and encourages immune response.

81%Digestibility after sprouting (vs. 41% dry)

Hydroponic Greenhouses & Hothouses

Grow more. In any climate.

Green Sprouts Solutions designs and manufactures hydroponic greenhouses and hothouses using our unique patented cladding material. Our structures are used by botanists, commercial flower growers, vegetable farmers and dedicated livestock farmers across Africa — particularly suited to cool, harsh or unpredictable climates where a controlled growing environment is not a luxury but a necessity.

We also produce unique hydroponic tunnels using our patented registered material. Greenhouses and tunnels can be custom designed to your specific requirement and application. We offer a fully modular concept — size varies according to your available space and growing needs, with standard widths from 3.0m up to 12.0m.

Botanists & Nurseries

Our hothouses provide the controlled environment that sensitive plants and seedlings require. Perfect for off-season propagation, hardening off seedlings, and growing high-value botanical species year-round regardless of outside conditions.

Commercial Flower Growers

Protect valuable cut flower crops from frost, wind, hail and excessive heat. Our structures allow you to grow premium flowers out of season, dramatically increasing the value and volume of your annual yield while reducing crop losses to unpredictable African weather.

Vegetable & Livestock Farmers

Make your farm drought-proof and weather-independent. Grow fresh vegetables and greens year-round without a pivot or ploughed field, using far less water and no heavy machinery. Combined with our Booster systems, a complete on-farm green feed solution is achievable for any farmer.

Patented Cladding Material

Built with material no one else has.

Our cladding material uses a unique patented formula and production process. The material is Polyethylene, compliant to ISO standards, UV-stabilised to deliver an exceptionally long service life in the African sun. Critically, it blocks out the harmful UV-rays that damage plants while transmitting the beneficial light spectrum plants need — making it ideal for growing across all weathering conditions from the Western Cape coast to the Highveld to tropical regions.

All material leaving our George factory goes through our strict in-house quality control system before despatch. Our patented registered material carries a guaranteed lifespan of 10 to 20 years — far outlasting conventional greenhouse plastics that degrade, yellow and tear within a few seasons.
