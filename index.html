Market Leader in Green Feed

Africa's most trusted fodder innovator.

Green Sprouts Solutions is a South African manufacturer of hydroponic fodder systems and hothouses, supplying farmers across Africa from our manufacturing facility in George, Western Cape. We have been engineering green feed solutions for over two decades, driven by a singular mission: to make high-quality, nutritious livestock feed accessible and affordable.

Our systems are designed to produce year-round greenfeed within 7 days without the need for a pivot or ploughing fields. We partner with university professors to develop precise feeding rations that maximize profit margins for our clients across different geographical regions.

Water Scarcity Solved

Water is becoming a scarce commodity. Irrigated land uses enormous amounts. Our standard booster system produces the equivalent of 15 hectares of irrigated land—redefining water efficiency for African agriculture and dramatically reducing your water costs.

Fungi & Mould Eliminated

By sticking to nature and using patented cladding that allows the right amount of sunlight through, combined with our organic bacterial product SporeBuster, we have all but eliminated moulding. Crucially, our system achieves temperature variation inside—impossible with air-conditioners—which is key for spore elimination.

University-Verified Nutrition

Sprouted fodder technology has had to overcome uneducated claims that livestock can thrive on sprouted fodder alone. We have found this to simply not be true. That is why we enrolled university professors to formulate precise feeding rations that include but are not limited to fodder. Rations are varied based on geographic location to reduce costs and increase client profit margins—redeeming the hydroponic fodder system through real science.

The Technology Behind It.

Answers to the questions every farmer asks.

No one can argue that artificial lighting cannot compare to natural sunlight in the chlorophyll process. Natural sunlight ensures healthier plants loaded with chlorophyll and vitamin D3—both essential elements for healthier livestock. By eliminating growing lights, we dramatically reduce electricity running costs and make solar integration far more cost-effective.

SporeBuster is our key organic bacterial product. Combined with our patented cladding technology, it allows the right amount of sunlight to reach the fodder while eliminating mould and fungal growth. A critical additional factor is that our system achieves temperature variation inside the booster—something that is impossible with a conventional air-conditioner—and this temperature variance is key to eliminating spores at the source.
