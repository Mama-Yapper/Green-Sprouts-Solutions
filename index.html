About Us

Grow good... in all aspects of life.

Green Sprouts Solutions has been around for more than 20 years, affording us the opportunity to become experts in sprouting technology and hothouse design.

Our Vision: To introduce a sufficient skills and development program to all the people of Africa, ensuring they become empowered to be self-sustainable.

Our Mission: By way of expertise and a positive spirit of enterprise and enthusiasm, we create products that will be beneficial in all spheres of life.

Founder Vivian Beukes set out with his engineering background and bacterial expertise to solve the most pressing issues faced by the industry. Today, farmers who have invested in our system agree it has revolutionized the way we feed livestock worldwide.
