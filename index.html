The Science of Sprouting

Why sprouted fodder works.

Seed Becomes Feed

A 50kg bag of seed sprouts into approximately 300kg of green, nutrient-dense fodder in just 6–7 days. The sprout mat is completely edible and highly nutritious as it is a living food — animals eat the entire mat, roots and green growth, so there is zero waste. Commonly used seeds are barley, wheat, maize and whole oats.

300kgYield from 50kg seed in 7 days

Dramatically Improved Digestibility

Barley's digestibility jumps from 41% to 81% through sprouting. Crude protein rises from 12.7% to 15.6% after 7 days of germination. Crude fibre increases from 5.5% to 14.2%, improving gut function. The action of sprouting also amplifies natural proteins, vitamins, minerals, enzymatic activity, omega-3, amino acids and natural hormones, and encourages immune response.

81%Digestibility after sprouting (vs. 41% dry)

Chlorophyll & Vitamin D3

Natural sunlight in our patented cladding system ensures plants are loaded with chlorophyll and vitamin D3 — both essential for healthier livestock. Artificial lighting cannot replicate this effect and creates a breeding ground for unwanted micro-organisms. There are several options to choose from, but the ideal are ones that are not temperature-controlled by an air-conditioner and ones that let natural sunlight carry out natural chlorophyll formation.

Hydroponic Greenhouses & Hothouses

Grow more. In any climate.

Green Sprouts Solutions manufactures hydroponic greenhouses and hothouses using our unique patented cladding material. Used by botanists, commercial flower growers, and dedicated farmers — particularly in cool or harsh environments where controlled growing is essential.

Patented Cladding System

Unique patented construction.

Our cladding material uses a unique patented formula and production process which enables us to produce durable sheets for our greenhouses. The material is Polyethylene, compliant to ISO standards, UV-stabilised to last very long in the sun, and blocks out harmful UV-rays that can damage plants — making it ideal for different weathering conditions.

We also focus on unique hydroponic tunnels manufactured with our patented registered material that guarantees a 10–20 year lifespan. Greenhouses can be custom designed according to your requirement and application. We offer a modular concept — size varies according to space available.
